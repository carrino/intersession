Not connected

An experiment: the board keeps a position by dead reckoning, integrating its acceleration twice, and each color reading paints a dot at that position. The square is 30 cm. Hold the board still for a moment so it knows its speed is zero, click Reset with it over the corner you want at the center, then move it screen-down an inch over the surface, pausing at the end of each stroke. It drifts within a few seconds, so expect a smear rather than a picture.

Not calibrated. Hold the board in the air, away from the desk, with each face toward the floor in turn. A face goes yellow when it is close, fills green while you hold still, and locks in after a second. Then set the board down on anything and leave it alone for a second: that measures the gyro.


    
The arrows are the accelerometer and magnetometer rotated into the room's frame by the orientation,
      so they should hold still while the board moves: down straight at the floor,
      mag toward magnetic north and down. If they swing with the board, the orientation is off.
      Once the board is calibrated, the mag fit keeps watching for anything the stored offset missed, so its
      offset should stay near zero. The color sensor only sees what is lit: hold button B to turn on the
      front LEDs while holding something in front of it.
