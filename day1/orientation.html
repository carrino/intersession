The arrows are the accelerometer and magnetometer rotated into the room's frame by the orientation,
      so they should hold still while the board moves: down straight at the floor,
      mag toward magnetic north and down. If they swing with the board, the orientation is off.
      Point each of the board's six faces at the floor in turn; once all 6 are covered the mag fit
      switches on and the offset is removed. The color sensor only sees what is lit: hold button B to turn on the
      front LEDs while holding something in front of it.
