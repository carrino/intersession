Not calibrated. Hold the board in the air, away from the desk, with each face toward the floor in turn. A face goes yellow when it is close, fills green while you hold still, and locks in after a second and a half. Then set the board down on anything and leave it alone: that measures the gyro. Click a card to clear it and do that one again.


    
The arrows are the accelerometer and magnetometer rotated into the room's frame by the orientation,
      so they should hold still while the board moves: down straight at the floor,
      mag toward magnetic north and down. If they swing with the board, the orientation is off.
      Once the board is calibrated, the mag fit keeps watching for anything the stored offset missed, so its
      offset should stay near zero. The color sensor only sees what is lit: hold button B to turn on the
      front LEDs while holding something in front of it. Selecting text in the table pauses it so it can be
      copied; click anywhere to let it run again.
